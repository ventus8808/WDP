TECHNICAL NOTES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Data Source:
• EQI 2000-2005 → AAMR 2006-2020 (lagged)
• N = 3,106 US counties
• Bayesian interval-censored spatiotemporal model
• 4,000 posterior draws per parameter

Interpretation:
• P-values: Posterior probabilities (Bayesian), NOT p-values
• Effects: Mortality rate differences vs. Q1 reference
• Positive = harmful, negative = protective
• Distributions show full uncertainty (not just point estimates)

Color-Blind Safe:
• Warm palette: Distinguishable in grayscale (intensity gradient)
• Cool palette: Also grayscale-safe
• Consider providing B&W version in supplement if required
